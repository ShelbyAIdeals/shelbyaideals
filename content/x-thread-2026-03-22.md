## Tweet 2

Pictory ($19/mo) — turns blog posts into short videos.

Not perfect. About 70% of the output is usable without heavy editing.

But for $19? If you're posting videos on LinkedIn or Instagram and don't want to learn Premiere Pro, it saves hours every week.

## Tweet 3

ElevenLabs (free to start) — AI voiceovers that actually sound human.

I've tried 6 voice tools. Nothing comes close.

I pair it with Pictory for videos. The combo costs $24/mo total and replaces what used to take me a full afternoon.

## Tweet 4

Frase ($15/mo) — SEO content optimization.

Surfer SEO does the same thing for $89/mo. Frase covers 80% of the functionality at 17% of the price.

If you're a solo writer or small team doing SEO content, this is the one.

## Tweet 5

Synthesia ($22/mo) — AI avatar videos.

I was skeptical. The avatars used to look weird.

Now? Surprisingly natural. If you need training videos, explainers, or onboarding content, it's way cheaper than hiring a presenter + renting a studio.

## Tweet 6

Tools I'd skip:

- Lumen5 ($29/mo) — more expensive than Pictory, worse output
- Midjourney — incredible images, but unless you're a designer, you won't use it enough
- Most "all-in-one" AI tools — they try to do everything and do nothing well
