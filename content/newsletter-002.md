Hey there,

Over the past few months, I spent 84 days testing 6 AI video tools back to back. Same workflow each time: I took a 1,200-word blog post and tried to turn it into a short video ready for social media.

Some tools nailed it. Some crashed and burned. Here's what I found.

---

### The Quick Verdict

| Tool | Best For | Starting Price | My Rating |
|------|----------|---------------|-----------|
| Pictory | Blog-to-video for social | $19/mo | 3.5/5 |
| Synthesia | Training and explainer videos | $22/mo | 4.0/5 |
| Descript | Podcast/video editing | $24/mo | 4.2/5 |

---

### What surprised me

**Pictory is the budget king.** At $19/mo, nothing else comes close for turning written content into video. The output isn't cinema-quality, but about 70% of the videos I generated were publish-worthy with minimal editing. For a small business posting 3-4 videos a week on LinkedIn or Instagram, that's a genuine time saver.

**Synthesia is better than I expected.** The AI avatars used to look uncanny. Now they're surprisingly natural. If you need training videos, onboarding content, or internal communications, Synthesia at $22/mo is significantly cheaper than hiring a presenter and renting a studio.

**Descript isn't really a "video creator" — it's an editor.** And it's the best AI-powered editor I've used. Record yourself talking, and it transcribes everything. Edit the text, and the video edits itself. Remove filler words with one click. If you already record content (podcasts, webinars, Loom recordings), Descript is the tool.

**ElevenLabs is the secret weapon.** It's technically a voice tool, not a video tool. But the voiceovers it generates sound genuinely human. I paired ElevenLabs audio with Pictory visuals and the result was better than any single all-in-one tool. The free tier gives you 10,000 characters/month to test.

**Lumen5 disappointed me.** At $29/mo, it's more expensive than Pictory but the output felt more generic. The stock footage selection was fine, but the AI didn't "understand" my content the way Pictory did. The enterprise features are solid if you're on a team, but for solopreneurs, there are better options.

---

### My recommendation by use case

**"I write blog posts and want video versions for social media"**
Go with Pictory ($19/mo). Fastest blog-to-video workflow I tested.

**"I need training or explainer videos with a presenter"**
Synthesia ($22/mo). The AI avatars are good enough to skip hiring actors.

**"I record podcasts or screen recordings and need to edit them"**
Descript ($24/mo). Edit video like a Google Doc.

**"I just need a great AI voice for narration"**
ElevenLabs (free to start). Best voice quality, works with any editor.

**"I need all of the above on a budget"**
Pictory + ElevenLabs = $24/mo total. That's my personal stack.
