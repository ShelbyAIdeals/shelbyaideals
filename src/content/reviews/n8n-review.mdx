## Direct Answer

**n8n is the automation platform to pick when your workflows are complex, AI-driven, or need to run on your own servers.** Cloud plans start at €20/month for 2,500 executions (billed annually), and the open-source Community Edition is free to self-host with no execution limits. Its billing model — one execution per workflow run, regardless of step count — is structurally cheaper than Zapier's per-task pricing for multi-step automations. The trade-off is a real learning curve.

## What Is n8n?

n8n (by n8n GmbH, Berlin) is a workflow automation platform with a visual node editor, source-available code, and first-class AI tooling: LLM nodes, agent frameworks, memory, and tool-calling are native primitives rather than bolted-on apps. You can run it two ways — n8n's cloud, or self-hosted from the free Community Edition (200k+ stars on GitHub).

Its direct competitors are [Zapier](/comparisons/zapier-vs-make) (largest app catalog, simplest), [Make](/reviews/make-review) (visual builder, cheapest per operation), and newer AI-workflow tools like Gumloop, Relay, Activepieces, and Pipedream.

## The Execution Model Is the Whole Story

Automation platforms meter differently, and it decides who should buy what:

| Platform | Billing unit | Entry paid plan | What a 15-step workflow run costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| n8n | 1 execution per run | €20/mo, 2,500 executions | 1 execution |
| Zapier | 1 task per step | $19.99/mo (annual), 750 tasks | 15 tasks |
| Make | 1 credit per module | $9/mo (annual), 10,000 credits | ~15 credits |

A 15-step AI agent running 50 times a day consumes ~1,500 n8n executions a month (fits Starter at €20) versus 22,500 Zapier tasks (a much higher tier). For simple 2-step zaps, the advantage evaporates — which is exactly the honest split: complexity favors n8n. Full three-way breakdown: [n8n vs Make vs Zapier](/comparisons/n8n-vs-make-vs-zapier).

## Best For / Not Ideal For

**Best for:**

- Developers and technical operators building multi-step, branching, retry-heavy workflows.
- AI-agent builders — LLM calls, tool use, and human-in-the-loop steps are native.
- Companies with data-residency requirements (self-hosted keeps data in-house).
- Anyone whose Zapier bill grew embarrassing as workflows gained steps.

**Not ideal for:**

- Nontechnical founders wiring "form → CRM → Slack" — [Zapier](/comparisons/zapier-vs-make) gets that live in minutes.
- Teams unwilling to maintain a server (use n8n cloud, or stay with Make/Zapier).
- Workflows that live and die by a niche SaaS integration only Zapier has.

## Pricing Reality Check

Verified plans are on our [n8n pricing page](/pricing/n8n): cloud Starter €20/month and Pro €50/month (both billed annually, free trial without a card), Business €667/month for self-hosted commercial features, custom Enterprise with 200+ concurrency. The Community Edition remains free with unlimited executions — your server, your uptime, your responsibility. That free path is n8n's most under-appreciated feature and the reason it dominates technical communities' recommendations.

## Limitations Worth Knowing

- **Learning curve is real.** The visual editor is approachable, but production workflows involve expressions, JSON wrangling, and debugging failed nodes.
- **Self-hosting is a job.** Updates, backups, queue scaling, and security patches are yours.
- **App catalog is smaller than Zapier's.** The generic HTTP/webhook nodes cover almost anything, at the cost of reading API docs yourself.
- **Cloud concurrency is capped by tier** (5 on Starter, 20 on Pro) — relevant for bursty webhook loads.

## Alternatives

- **[Make](/reviews/make-review)** — cheapest per unit of work ($9/month for 10,000 credits), strong visual builder. [Make pricing](/pricing/make-com).
- **Zapier** — largest integration catalog, easiest start, priciest per task. See [Zapier vs Make](/comparisons/zapier-vs-make).
- **Gumloop / Relay / Activepieces / Pipedream** — younger AI-workflow platforms; not yet profiled by us.

## Final Recommendation

Choose **n8n** if you are technical and your automations are multi-step or AI-driven — start on the free Community Edition if you can host, or cloud Starter (€20/month) if you cannot. Choose **Make** if you want the lowest cost for straightforward automations. Choose **Zapier** if setup speed and app coverage beat cost for you.
