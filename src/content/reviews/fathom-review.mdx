## Direct Answer

**Fathom is the AI meeting notetaker with the strongest free plan in the category: unlimited recordings, transcriptions, and instant AI summaries at $0, with both bot and bot-free capture.** Paid tiers exist for advanced summaries (Premium, $16/month annual) and sales teams (Business, $25/user annual with CRM sync and coaching). If your priority is integrations and searchable transcript archives, [Fireflies](/reviews/fireflies-review) is the alternative to weigh; if it is real-time transcription UX, [Otter](/reviews/otter-ai-review).

## What Is Fathom?

Fathom (Fathom Video Inc.) is an AI meeting assistant for Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams: it records, transcribes, summarizes, and extracts action items from calls. Two design choices define it — the permanently free unlimited core, and capture that can work without adding a visible bot participant to your meeting.

Its direct competitors are [Otter.ai](/reviews/otter-ai-review), [Fireflies.ai](/reviews/fireflies-review), and Granola (the notes-app-style assistant without a bot; not yet profiled by us). The three-way comparison of the incumbents is here: [Otter vs Fireflies vs Fathom](/comparisons/otter-vs-fireflies-vs-fathom).

## The Free Plan Is the Product

Meeting AI free tiers, verified August 2026:

| Tool | Free plan reality |
|---|---|
| Fathom | Unlimited recordings, transcriptions, AI summaries |
| Otter | 300 min/month, 30 min per conversation |
| Fireflies | Unlimited transcription, 400 min storage/team |

For an individual who just wants every meeting captured and summarized, that table ends the discussion — Fathom free does the whole job with no meter running. The catch is deliberate: **advanced** summary formats, AI action items, and the ask-your-meeting assistant live in Premium ($20/month, $16 annual, 90-day guarantee).

## Best For / Not Ideal For

**Best for:**

- Individuals who want meeting notes handled, permanently, for free.
- People whose clients dislike bot participants — bot-free capture is rare in this category.
- Sales teams: Business ($25/user annual) syncs CRM fields automatically, tracks deals, and scores calls against coaching metrics.

**Not ideal for:**

- Teams that live in integrations (Salesforce/HubSpot/Slack/Notion pipelines) — [Fireflies](/reviews/fireflies-review) has the broader catalog and an API.
- Heavy archive searchers — Fireflies Business offers unlimited storage with deep search.
- Real-time-transcript-on-screen workflows — Otter's live view remains the reference.

## Pricing Reality Check

Full verified table on the [Fathom pricing page](/pricing/fathom): Free forever; Premium $20/month ($16 annual); Team $19/user ($15 annual, 2+ users); Business $34/user ($25 annual). Annual billing saves 25%+, and all paid plans carry a 90-day guarantee — unusually long for the category.

## Limitations Worth Knowing

- The free tier's summaries are the standard template — the flexible/advanced formats are Premium.
- Team collaboration (shared folders, global search, keyword alerts) starts at 2+ paid seats.
- Compliance features (SSO, SCIM, retention control) are Enterprise-only.

## Alternatives

- **[Otter.ai](/reviews/otter-ai-review)** — real-time transcription, $8.33/month annual Pro. [Pricing](/pricing/otter-ai).
- **[Fireflies.ai](/reviews/fireflies-review)** — integrations and storage, $10/month annual Pro. [Pricing](/pricing/fireflies).
- **Granola** — bot-free notes-first assistant (not yet profiled by us).

## Final Recommendation

Start with **Fathom free** — for most individuals it simply removes the meeting-notes problem at $0. Upgrade to **Premium** only when the basic summaries stop being enough. Buy **Business** if you run a sales team and want CRM hygiene automated. Pick **Fireflies** over Fathom when integrations and transcript archives are the actual job, and **Otter** when live transcription is.
