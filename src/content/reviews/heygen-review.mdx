## Direct Answer

**HeyGen is the strongest avatar-video generator for marketing content and localization, priced from $29/month (Creator, $24 annual).** Its edge over Synthesia is avatar realism and per-language lip-synced translation; its weakness is a credit system where the newest avatar models cost roughly six times more per minute than standard ones. If your work is corporate training libraries with centralized management, Synthesia fits better at nearly the same price.

## What Is HeyGen?

HeyGen is an AI video platform by HeyGen Inc. that generates presenter-style videos from a script: you pick an avatar (stock, photo-based, or a "digital twin" of yourself), paste text, and get a talking video in 175+ languages. Its three core jobs are marketing videos, localized versions of existing footage (translation with re-rendered lip-sync), and spokesperson-style product content.

Its main competitors are [Synthesia](/reviews/synthesia-review) (corporate training focus), Colossyan (training), and — for ad-specific UGC video — Creatify and Arcads. For cinematic text-to-video, tools like [Runway](/reviews/runway-ml-review), Kling, Sora, and Veo are a different category: HeyGen renders presenters, not scenes.

## How HeyGen Pricing Actually Works

The published plans ([verified breakdown](/pricing/heygen)) are simple; the credit system is where budgets go wrong:

| Plan | Price | Credits | Key limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | — | 3 videos/mo, 1 min each |
| Creator | $29/mo ($24 annual) | 600/mo | 1080p, videos to 30 min |
| Pro | $49/mo | 1,000/mo | 4K, all advanced models |
| Business | $149/mo + $20/seat | 1,500/mo | 60-min videos, SSO, 5 twins |

Per HeyGen's own rate card, a minute of standard studio avatar video (Avatar III) costs **3 credits**, while the newest Avatar IV/V models cost **20 credits per minute**, and video translation runs 2–5 credits per minute. On Creator that means either ~200 minutes of standard avatar video or ~30 minutes on flagship models per month. Decide which model tier your content actually needs before choosing a plan.

## Best For / Not Ideal For

**Best for:**

- Marketing teams producing short presenter videos weekly.
- Localization workflows — one master video re-rendered into many languages with matching lip-sync.
- Founders and creators using a personal digital twin for content at scale.
- Ecommerce brands making spokesperson-style product explainers.

**Not ideal for:**

- Structured corporate training libraries with SCORM/LMS-centric workflows — [Synthesia](/reviews/synthesia-review) is built around exactly that.
- Cinematic or B-roll generation — wrong category.
- One-off video needs — the free plan covers a test, but a single project may not justify a subscription.

## HeyGen vs Synthesia (Short Version)

The two are priced almost identically at entry ($29/month each; $24 vs $18 on annual billing). HeyGen meters monthly credits with per-model rates; Synthesia meters minutes pooled per year. HeyGen leads on avatar realism and translation breadth; Synthesia on training-video structure, brand management, and enterprise compliance. The full breakdown with a scenario table is in our [HeyGen vs Synthesia comparison](/comparisons/heygen-vs-synthesia).

## Limitations Worth Knowing

- **Model-dependent cost:** the 3-vs-20-credits-per-minute spread means "how much does a video cost" has no single answer. Estimate with your target model.
- **Presenter format ceiling:** avatars talk to camera. Complex scenes, motion graphics, and edits still need a video editor ([Descript](/reviews/descript-review) pairs well).
- **Custom digital twins are tiered:** 1 on Free, more only on Business (5) and Enterprise (10+).
- **API is priced separately** from the subscription plans.

## Alternatives

- **[Synthesia](/reviews/synthesia-review)** — corporate training, yearly minute pools, 140+ languages. See also [Synthesia alternatives](/alternatives/synthesia).
- **Colossyan** — training-focused avatar video (not yet profiled by us).
- **Creatify** — UGC-style ad videos rather than presenter content ([pricing](/pricing/creatify)).
- **[Pictory](/reviews/pictory-review)** — script/blog to stock-footage video without avatars, from $25/month.

## Final Recommendation

Choose **HeyGen** if avatar realism, photo avatars, or multilingual re-rendering of one video are your priorities — start on Creator ($29/month) and only move up when flagship-model credits run short. Choose **Synthesia** if you are building a structured training library that a team maintains. Choose **neither** if you need cinematic text-to-video — that is Runway/Kling/Sora territory.
