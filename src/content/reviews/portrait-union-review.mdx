## What Is Portrait Union?

Portrait Union is an online studio that paints a portrait from one photo of a pet or a person, in one of twelve styles, using an image model. It also restores old photographs: scratches, creases and fading are repaired and the picture is returned in colour. You upload one photo, get one free watermarked preview in under a minute, and pay only if you want to keep it: a digital file is €4.99 and a gallery canvas starts at €69.99 with shipping included.

There is no prompt box, no account and no subscription. You choose who is in the photo and a style; the studio does the rest. The site states plainly that every portrait and restoration is made by an image model, not painted by a person, and that the pictures it shows are samples rather than customer work.

## Who It Suits

- **Pet owners.** Dogs, cats, rabbits, horses and other four-legged pets keep their breed, markings, fur and eye colour, and stay on four legs in their own bodies; costumes are draped over the animal rather than replacing it. Several pets in one photo cost no more.
- **Couples and families.** One photo of two people, or of the whole family, becomes one painting with every face kept, the dog included if it was in the picture.
- **Gift buyers.** Every print includes the digital file, so a file can be given on the day while the canvas is on its way.
- **Anyone with an old print.** A phone photo of a creased or faded print can be restored and colourised, then ordered as a canvas.

### Not a fit for

- **Power users.** No prompts, no seeds, no API and no batch mode.
- **Other kinds of images.** Portraits of pets and people and photo restoration only.
- **Custom proportions for portraits.** Painted portraits are composed at 4:5. Restorations keep their photograph's own proportions.
- **People who want a hand-painted original.** These are model-made images.

## The Twelve Styles

Renaissance Royal, Floral Garden, Lavender Field, Watercolor, Greek God, Samurai Warrior, GTA Style, Astronaut Explorer, Van Gogh Style, Gothic Noble, Hollywood Premiere and Mafia Boss. Every style paints pets and people alike. Van Gogh Style is the one that keeps the clothes you were photographed in; the others dress the subject for the style.

## How It Works

1. **Upload one photo** (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 15 MB) and choose who is in it and a style.
2. **See a free preview** in under a minute: one watermarked, low-resolution picture with 400 px of detail on the long edge, on a private link that stays open for 48 hours.
3. **Keep it or walk away.** Buy the watermark-free file or a print beside the preview; download links arrive by email right after payment.

Each visitor gets one free preview per day, one photo in one style. A purchase adds another free preview the same day.

## Pricing

Every price is a one-off purchase; there is no subscription and there are no credit packs.

- **Digital file: €4.99** ($4.99, £3.99), for a portrait or a restoration.
- **Gallery canvas for a portrait:** 20×25 cm €69.99, 40×50 cm €119.99, 60×75 cm €179.99.
- **Framed canvas for a portrait:** 20×25 cm €129.99, 40×50 cm €179.99, in black, dark brown or natural wood.
- **Restorations** add landscape, square, 2:3 and 3:4 canvas sizes, from €69.99. Only sizes that keep the total crop of the original within 6.5% are offered, and the exact dimensions are shown beside the preview before payment.

Prices are shown in euros; buyers in the United Kingdom pay in pounds and buyers in the United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Singapore and South Korea pay in US dollars. Every print price includes shipping and the digital file.

## Files and Print Quality

A portrait file is a 2048 × 2560 px JPEG at 4:5, yours to keep, print and use commercially. That is 260 dpi at 20×25 cm, 130 dpi at 40×50 cm and 87 dpi at 60×75 cm; every print file is enlarged once before production. A restoration is delivered at the resolution the restoration model produces, about 4 megapixels. Gallery canvas wraps 49 mm around each side of the frame.

## Shipping, Delivery and Returns

Prints are produced by Gelato at the facility nearest the delivery address and shipped to 35 countries with shipping included. Production takes 3–5 business days, then 2–5 days in transit within Europe, 2–4 days to the United Kingdom and 4–9 days overseas; the site suggests allowing two to three weeks in total.

A digital file is delivered immediately, and the 14-day right of withdrawal lapses once the download begins. Prints are made to order; a voluntary return is accepted within 14 days of delivery at the buyer's postage cost, and damaged prints are replaced free of charge.

## How It Compares

Against general image generators such as [Midjourney](/reviews/midjourney-review) or [Leonardo AI](/reviews/leonardo-ai-review), the trade is range for a guided path: those tools draw anything and give you control, while Portrait Union does one job and ends in a file or a shipped canvas. Portrait Union is for people who want a painted portrait of a pet or a person, or a restored family photograph, without learning a tool. The free preview of your own photo is the honest test; nothing on this page is a substitute for it.
