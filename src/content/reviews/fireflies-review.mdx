## Direct Answer

**Fireflies.ai is the meeting assistant to choose when integrations are the job: transcripts and summaries that flow into Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Notion, and 50+ other tools automatically, from $10/month on annual billing.** Every plan has unlimited transcription; the tiers sell storage and admin. If you need none of that plumbing, [Fathom's](/reviews/fathom-review) unlimited free plan is the better deal; if you want live on-screen transcription, [Otter](/reviews/otter-ai-review).

*This is a researched profile: facts verified against official sources (pricing checked August 18, 2026). Our hands-on protocol has not run on Fireflies yet — no ShelbyAI score until it does.*

## What Is Fireflies.ai?

Fireflies.ai (Fireflies Inc.) is an AI meeting assistant whose bot ("Fred") joins Zoom, Meet, and Teams calls, transcribes them, produces summaries and action items, and — its differentiator — pushes those artifacts wherever your team works. The AskFred assistant answers questions across your meeting archive, which grows more useful as storage accumulates.

Direct competitors: [Otter.ai](/reviews/otter-ai-review), [Fathom](/reviews/fathom-review), and Granola. Three-way breakdown: [Otter vs Fireflies vs Fathom](/comparisons/otter-vs-fireflies-vs-fathom).

## Pricing: Unlimited Transcription, Metered Storage

Verified table on the [Fireflies pricing page](/pricing/fireflies): Free (400 min team storage), Pro $18/month or $10 annual (8,000 min storage per seat), Business $29/$19 (unlimited storage), Enterprise $39 annual-only. Annual billing saves up to 44% — the honest buying advice is that monthly Fireflies is overpriced relative to its own annual rate.

The free plan deserves a plain description: transcription is unlimited, but 400 minutes of *storage per team* means roughly two weeks of daily meetings before old transcripts must go. Treat it as an evaluation window.

## Best For / Not Ideal For

**Best for:**

- Sales and customer teams pushing notes into CRMs automatically.
- Teams that search old meetings often (Business's unlimited storage + AskFred).
- Ops teams wiring meetings into Zapier/API workflows.

**Not ideal for:**

- Individuals without integration needs — Fathom free is unlimited where Fireflies free is storage-capped.
- Bot-averse meetings — Fireflies always joins as a participant.
- Live-transcript-while-talking workflows — Otter's real-time view leads.

## Limitations Worth Knowing

- Storage caps, not transcription caps, are the real meter — size your plan by meeting volume retained, not recorded.
- Advanced AI features draw on per-plan AI credits (20/30/50).
- Enterprise is annual-billing only.

## Alternatives

- **[Fathom](/reviews/fathom-review)** — unlimited free core, bot-free capture, sales-team Business tier. [Pricing](/pricing/fathom).
- **[Otter.ai](/reviews/otter-ai-review)** — real-time transcription, cheapest annual Pro ($8.33/month). [Pricing](/pricing/otter-ai).
- **Granola** — no-bot, notes-first approach (not yet profiled by us).

## Final Recommendation

Choose **Fireflies Pro (annual, $10/month)** when meeting notes must reach your CRM and team tools without human effort — nothing else in the category does that plumbing as well at the price. Choose **Fathom** when you want unlimited notes for free, and **Otter** when live transcription matters most.
