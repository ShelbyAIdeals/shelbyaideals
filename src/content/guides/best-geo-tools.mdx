## What GEO Software Does (and Doesn't)

Generative Engine Optimization tools answer one question continuously: *when buyers ask AI assistants the questions that used to be Google searches, does your brand appear in the answers?*

Concretely, they provide:

- **Prompt tracking** — your defined buyer questions, run against AI engines on schedule
- **Mention tracking** — your brand named in answer text
- **Citation tracking** — your pages linked as sources
- **Competitor visibility** — who else those answers recommend, and how often
- **Engine coverage** — the same prompt across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Copilot, and (as add-ons) Claude and Gemini
- **Prompt discovery** — surfacing question phrasings you didn't think to track
- **Reporting** — share-of-voice trends your team or clients can read

What they do **not** do is change the answers. That work is content: extractable facts, verified pricing, honest comparisons, structured data. The tool is the scoreboard.

## The Standalone Tools

### Otterly.AI — the accessible entry ($29/mo)

Lite at $29/month ($25 annual) tracks 15 prompts daily across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot, with unlimited team members. Standard ($189/month) moves to 100 prompts and adds API/MCP access; Premium ($489) to 400 prompts. Claude, Gemini, and Google AI Mode are paid add-ons ($9-439/month by plan). For a single brand watching its core queries, nothing else delivers multi-engine daily data at this price. Full plan table: [Otterly pricing](/pricing/otterly).

### Profound — the analytical deep end ($99-399/mo)

Profound meters responses, not just prompts: Starter ($99/month, billed yearly) samples 50 prompts with 1,500 monthly responses — but on ChatGPT only. The representative tier is Growth ($399/month): 100 prompts, 9,000 responses, three engines. Enterprise adds up to nine engines, SOC 2, SSO, and multi-company tracking. The response-volume approach matters because AI answers are volatile — one query per day can miss what a hundred samples catch. Full plan table: [Profound pricing](/pricing/profound).

### Peec AI — pricing behind sales

Peec targets SEO and content teams with Starter/Pro/Advanced/Enterprise tiers on annual billing, but publishes no prices. That is not disqualifying — it is simply unverifiable from public sources, so trial it head-to-head against Otterly or Profound on your own prompt set and make sales quote against the numbers above.
