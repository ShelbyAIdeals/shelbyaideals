## n8n vs Make vs Zapier: Quick Verdict

**Pick by workflow complexity and technical comfort: n8n for complex or AI-driven workflows (and self-hosting), Make for cheap straightforward automations, Zapier for the fastest setup and widest app coverage.**

| Scenario | Winner | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AI-agent / multi-step workflows | n8n | Per-execution billing + native LLM nodes |
| Nontechnical founders | Zapier | Easiest setup, biggest catalog |
| Budget, simple automations | Make | $9/mo for 10,000 credits |
| Data control / self-hosting | n8n | Free unlimited Community Edition |
| Niche app coverage | Zapier | Thousands of integrations |
| Best free tier | n8n | Unlimited self-hosted; Make best hosted free |

## Pricing: Three Different Meters

The platforms don't just charge different amounts — they count different things:

| | n8n | Make.com | Zapier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Billing unit | Execution (whole workflow run) | Credit (each module run) | Task (each action step) |
| Free tier | Community Edition: unlimited, self-hosted | 1,000 credits/mo hosted | 100 tasks/mo, 2-step Zaps |
| Entry paid | Starter €20/mo annual, 2,500 executions | Core $9/mo annual, 10,000 credits | Pro from $19.99/mo annual, 750 tasks |
| Mid tier | Pro €50/mo, 10,000 executions | Pro $16/mo, priority execution | Pro 2,000 tasks $49/mo annual |
| Team tier | Business €667/mo (self-hosted features) | Teams $29/mo | Team from $69/mo annual |
| Currency | EUR | USD | USD |

**Worked example — a 15-step AI workflow running 50 times per day (~1,500 runs/month):**

- **n8n:** 1,500 executions → fits Starter at €20/month.
- **Make:** ~22,500 credits → needs a higher-volume Core tier.
- **Zapier:** 22,500 tasks → well past the $129/month 10,000-task Pro tier.

Reverse the example for a 2-step automation running 500 times a month and the ranking flips: Make's $9 plan is the cheapest, Zapier's free tier might even cover it, and n8n's advantage disappears. **Step count is the deciding variable.**

## Who Should Pick What

**Pick n8n** if you're technical, your workflows involve AI models, branching, retries, or approval steps — or if data must stay on your infrastructure. Start free self-hosted, or cloud Starter at €20/month. Details: [n8n review](/reviews/n8n-review) · [n8n pricing](/pricing/n8n).

**Pick Make** if your automations are conventional (sync, notify, create records) and you want the lowest bill: $9/month buys 10,000 credits, and the visual canvas handles moderate complexity well. Details: [Make review](/reviews/make-review) · [Make pricing](/pricing/make-com).

**Pick Zapier** if you're nontechnical, value your time over the subscription cost, or depend on niche apps. The two-way breakdown with Make is here: [Zapier vs Make](/comparisons/zapier-vs-make).

**Running two is legitimate:** plenty of teams keep Zapier for simple glue and n8n for the heavy AI workflows — the combined bill is often lower than forcing everything through one platform's pricing model.

## The Bottom Line

The automation market has sorted itself: **Zapier sells convenience, Make sells capacity, n8n sells power and control.** Price per unit only matters after you know which unit your workflows consume — count the steps in your three most important automations and the winner usually announces itself.
