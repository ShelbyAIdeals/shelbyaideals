## 1. First, the thing that changed

**OpenAI retired DALL-E 2 and DALL-E 3 on 12 May 2026.** If you landed here looking for a DALL-E comparison, that product no longer exists.

OpenAI has since shipped **GPT Image 2.5** (8 September 2026) on top of it — see our [GPT Image 2 vs GPT Image 2.5 comparison](/comparisons/gpt-image-2-vs-gpt-image-2-5) for that split.

What replaced it is not a like-for-like successor. OpenAI folded image generation into ChatGPT itself: **GPT Image 1.5** quietly took over as the default image model in December 2025, and **ChatGPT Images 2.0** rolled out in April 2026 on the **`gpt-image-2`** model. There is no separate DALL-E product to subscribe to any more — image generation is a feature of your ChatGPT plan.

That reframes this comparison. It is no longer three image tools; it is **two dedicated image tools and one feature bundled into a subscription most marketers already pay for.**

Choose **Leonardo AI** when you need a genuinely free tier or predictable high-volume token budgeting, and check the licensing terms before you publish anything from its free plan.

## 4. The three cost models are not comparable

This is the part most comparisons get wrong. These three tools do not sell the same unit, so a "price per image" table is misleading. Here is what you are actually buying:

| Tool | You buy | Entry price | What that gets you |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Midjourney** | Fast GPU time | $10/mo (Basic) | 3.3 hours fast GPU/month |
| **GPT Image 2** | A ChatGPT plan | $0 free / $8 Go / $20 Plus | Image generation with plan-scaled caps |
| **Leonardo AI** | Tokens | $0 free / $12 (Apprentice) | 150 tokens/day free; 8,500 tokens/mo paid |

### Midjourney

| Plan | Monthly | Annual (per month) | Fast GPU time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $10 | $8 | 3.3 hours |
| Standard | $30 | $24 | 15 hours + unlimited Relax mode |
| Pro | $60 | $48 | 30 hours |
| Mega | $120 | $96 | 60 hours |

Annual billing cuts each plan by 20%. **There is no free plan and no free trial** — Midjourney is the only one of the three you cannot try without paying. The Standard plan's *unlimited Relax mode* is the detail that matters for marketers: once you exhaust fast GPU time, you can keep generating at lower priority instead of stopping.

### GPT Image 2

Included in ChatGPT: **Free** (limited, and carrying ads as of February 2026), **Go** at $8/month, **Plus** at $20/month, and higher Pro, Business and Enterprise tiers. Usage caps scale with the plan.

Via the API, `gpt-image-2` bills per token: **$5 per million text input tokens, $8 per million image input tokens, and $30 per million image output tokens** (cached input is discounted) — which independent calculators put at roughly **$0.03-$0.08 per image** depending on size and quality.

For a marketer, the honest framing is that **GPT Image 2 is free at the point of use** if you already pay $20/month for ChatGPT Plus for writing and analysis. That is a hard economic argument for the other two to beat.

### Leonardo AI

| Plan | Monthly | Tokens |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 150/day (~4,500/month) |
| Apprentice | $12 | 8,500/month |
| Artisan | $30 | 25,000/month |
| Maestro | $60 | 60,000/month |

Annual billing carries a discount that varies by plan. Apprentice adds private generations and the Consistent Character Engine; Artisan adds custom model training and real-time canvas; Maestro raises concurrency and queue priority.

**Read this before using the free tier commercially:** Leonardo's free plan excludes private image storage, the Consistent Character Engine, custom model training and API access — and every image generated on it is **publicly visible and owned by Leonardo AI**. That is fine for experimenting. It is not fine for client work or brand assets. See our [Leonardo AI pricing breakdown](/pricing/leonardo-ai) for the full plan detail.

## 5. Midjourney: strengths and weaknesses

Midjourney remains the quality benchmark. It is the only one of the three sold purely as an image generator, and it shows in the output — particularly on stylised, editorial and photorealistic work where the aesthetic *is* the deliverable.

**Strengths**
- **Output quality:** consistently the most visually accomplished results of the three
- **Style control:** deep parameter and reference control for art direction
- **Relax mode:** unlimited lower-priority generation from the $30 Standard plan up

**Weaknesses**
- **No free tier at all** — you cannot evaluate it without paying
- **GPU-hour billing is hard to forecast** against a monthly content plan
- **Learning curve:** parameters and prompt craft take real time to master
- **Text rendering** is its weakest area, and marketing graphics are full of text

## 6. GPT Image 2: strengths and weaknesses

The successor to DALL-E, and a very different proposition: it lives inside ChatGPT rather than standing alone.

**Strengths**
- **Already paid for** if you subscribe to ChatGPT — no separate tool, login or invoice
- **Best text rendering of the three**, which is the single most common failure mode in AI-generated ad creative, thumbnails and social graphics
- **Conversational editing:** you iterate in the same thread you wrote the brief in
- **A genuinely usable free tier**, unlike Midjourney

**Weaknesses**
- **Not a professional image tool:** no equivalent to Midjourney's style parameters or Leonardo's custom model training
- **Caps are opaque** — they scale with your plan rather than being published as a number you can budget against
- **Free tier carries ads** as of February 2026
- **Verify rights before publishing.** Check OpenAI's current terms for commercial use rather than assuming; the licensing position has moved more than once since 2025

## 7. Leonardo AI: strengths and weaknesses

The middle option, and the one with the clearest cost model.

**Strengths**
- **Real free tier** at 150 tokens a day
- **Token allowances are explicit** — you can forecast spend against a content calendar
- **Custom model training** on Artisan and above, for brand-consistent output
- **Consistent Character Engine** from Apprentice up, useful for recurring brand mascots or personas

**Weaknesses**
- **Free-tier images are public and owned by Leonardo AI** — a genuine blocker for client work
- **Output quality trails Midjourney** on demanding aesthetic work
- **Tokens are a second currency to manage** alongside everything else in your stack

## 9. Real-world scenarios

**You already pay for ChatGPT Plus.** Use GPT Image 2 and buy nothing else until it visibly fails you. For social graphics, blog headers and ad concepts it is sufficient, and the text rendering advantage is real.

**You are a freelance designer and the image is the deliverable.** Midjourney Standard at $30/month. The unlimited Relax mode matters more than the fast GPU hours once you are iterating heavily.

**You need brand-consistent characters or products across dozens of assets.** Leonardo AI Artisan at $30/month, for custom model training and the Consistent Character Engine. Neither competitor offers this.

**You are testing whether AI images belong in your workflow at all.** Leonardo's free tier or ChatGPT's — but do not publish free-tier Leonardo output, because you do not own it.

**You are generating images programmatically at volume.** GPT Image 2 via the API, at roughly $0.03-$0.08 per image, or Leonardo's API on a paid plan. Midjourney has no official public API.

## 10. Final recommendation

**Start with what you already pay for.** The most common mistake in 2026 is buying a dedicated image tool while an adequate one sits unused inside an existing ChatGPT subscription.

Escalate from there: add **Midjourney** ($10-30/month) when output quality is genuinely the constraint, and **Leonardo AI** ($12-30/month) when you need custom-trained models, consistent characters, or a token budget you can forecast.

And if you came here for DALL-E: it is gone, its replacement is bundled into ChatGPT, and for most marketing work that replacement is good enough.
